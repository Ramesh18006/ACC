Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At Aruna Computers Care (ACC), we prioritize the privacy and security of the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy details the information we collect and how we utilize it to bring you superior IT service.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the absolute necessary inputs required to provide our repair services and product deliveries, including: Name, Phone Number, Email, and physical addresses (for on-site service requirements). We also collect device specifications when submitted via our chatbot or forms.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for internal logistics: communicating via our automated Make/Twilio nodes for service tracking, estimating checkout prices, and issuing invoices. Your data is never sold or rented to third-party data brokers.

3. Data Security

Information submitted is encrypted in transit and securely hosted on our high-performance Supabase nodes. Due to our zero-OTP architecture, your personal dashboard is linked safely to your local device state.
